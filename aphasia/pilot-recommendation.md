# Aphasia pilot: recommendation for Friday Oct 23

Context: Peter's file has ~130 donors with phone numbers out of ~1,400 appeal recipients. He offered two options: phone append, or call the 130 and monitor.

## The case

**A randomized split of 130 proves nothing.** 65 called vs 65 not called needs a 21-point lift in gift rate to show significance (see pilot-stats.md). A thank-you call does not do that. The test would report "no difference" even if it worked, and that result would get quoted.

**Phone append doesn't fix it either.** Best case adds ~500 numbers, which gets the detectable lift to ~9 points. Still large. And appended numbers come with three costs: Aphasia pays for them, match rates in Canada are low and skew landline, and calling a number the donor never gave the charity is the one version of this that creates PIPEDA and "how did you get this number" risk (see phone-append-research.md). It also mixes two donor populations in one test.

**Call all 130. No split.** Reframe the pilot around what 130 can prove:
- Experience: pickup rate, call length, sentiment, opt-outs, complaints, accessibility handling
- Operations: did the system run clean for a week, did transfers and callbacks work, did the report land with Mobbs
- Signal: appeal response of the 130 vs their own last year, and vs the rest of the list, reported as descriptive not causal
- Consent capture: SMS opt-in and cell numbers collected for next year

That is Chris's retention and relationship story. It is also what a year-two lift test needs: more consented numbers.

## Three designs side by side

| | (a) Call all 130 | (b) Append then 50/50 | (c) 130 now, append in spring |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proves | experience, ops, descriptive signal | lift only if effect is 9+ pts | (a) now, option on (b) later |
| Can't prove | causal lift | anything if effect is small | same as (a) now |
| Retell cost at $0.21/min, 1.5 min | ~$41 | ~$200 plus append fee | ~$41 now |
| Append fee | none | Aphasia pays, quote needed | deferred |
| Tan hours | ~12 build + 4 run | ~20 | ~12 now |
| Risk | low | PIPEDA + sentiment on appended numbers | low |

## The one question Chris must settle with Peter
Is the pilot's success metric "donors felt good and nothing broke" or "gift rate went up"? With 130 numbers it can only be the first. If Peter needs the second, the pilot is a year-two project after this year's calls collect consented numbers.
