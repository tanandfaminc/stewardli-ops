# LinkedIn prospecting ... how Chris uses it

Owner: Chris. Tan supports. Built from prompts/C-linkedin-prospecting.md.

This is a list and drafts. Nothing here connects or messages anyone. You send every note by hand from your own LinkedIn.

## What's in a batch
`contacts-batch-NN.csv`, one row per person:

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| org | Organization name |
| contact | Person named on a public page |
| role | Their role as that page shows it |
| score | Fit, 4 or 5 only (see below) |
| source URL | Where the name and role came from. Check it before you send |

Rows to check first in batch 01: Parkdale (ED named in a 2023 source; CanadaHelps says Kathleen, notes say Kitty) and Ernestine's (Laverne Blake was interim ED as of Oct 2025).
| note v1, v2, v3 | Three connection notes, each under 300 characters |

## How to use it (10 minutes a day)
1. Open the CSV in Google Sheets. Add two columns of your own: `sent` (date) and `reply`.
2. Take five rows a day. More than that and the notes stop sounding like you.
3. For each row, open the source URL. Check the person is still in that role. If not, skip the row and mark it `gone`.
4. Find them on LinkedIn by name and org. Check it's the same person.
5. Pick the note that sounds most like you. Edit freely. Read it out loud once.
6. Click Connect, then "Add a note", paste, send. By hand, every time.
7. When they accept, wait a day. Then a short thank you. Still no pitch. The stewardship conversation comes when they ask what you're working on.

## Rules the notes follow
- Warm, direct, peer to peer. You're a fundraiser and board member writing to another one.
- One specific thing about their org, taken from a public page.
- No pitch, no product, no AI in the first note.
- Positioning, when it comes up later: stewardship, not operations.

## How rows were picked
Sources: CharityVillage job postings, CanadaHelps charity profiles, Ontario Nonprofit Network. Imagine Canada blocks automated reads, so it's not in batch 01.

Batch 01 is 6 rows, not 25. 39 orgs were collected and scored; only 6 scored 4 or 5. The gap was named contacts: charity staff pages couldn't be read from the build environment, and most job postings say "reports to the Executive Director" without a name. Batch 02 reads staff pages directly and starts from 75 Ontario fundraising postings already found.

Fit score, 1 to 5, on three things:
- Small enough that they don't have a stewardship team
- Has an annual appeal or event
- Has a named fundraising contact (or ED or board chair at a small shop)

Only 4s and 5s make the batch.

Left out on purpose: Fred's leads, current clients and pipeline (Aphasia Institute, U of T, the Melanoma org, TSI), Humber, hospitals and universities.

## Before you send anything
- **Check against Fred's list.** His leads are his. If an org is on his list, delete the row.
- Names come from public pages and can be out of date. The source URL is there so you can check.
- Don't paste this CSV into GHL or anywhere else. It's a working list, not a contact import.
