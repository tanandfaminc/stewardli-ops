# Nora calling nonprofit executives (B2B sales outreach): conditions

Status: research for Tan. Researched 2026-11-26. Not legal advice. LAWYER items need review before any live call.

Use case: Nora (AI voice, Retell) calls an ED or development director at a Canadian nonprofit, on a number they publish, to pitch Stewardli.

## Bottom line

Nora cannot cold call these executives to sell. A sales call by an AI voice is very likely an ADAD solicitation call, and that needs the person's **prior express consent**. The B2B exemption only takes you out of the National DNCL. It does not take you out of the ADAD rules. A public phone number is not consent.

## 1. National DNCL: exempt

- Calls to business consumers are exempt from Part II (National DNCL Rules) [1][2]. No list scrub needed.
- Parts III (Telemarketing Rules) and IV (ADAD Rules) still apply to business numbers [2].
- Do not rely on the charity exemption. That covers calls made by or for a registered charity asking for donations. Stewardli selling to a charity is ordinary telemarketing.
- LAWYER: whether Stewardli must still register with the DNCL operator as an exempt telemarketer.

## 2. ADAD rules: the blocker

- ADAD = equipment that dials stored numbers and conveys a "pre-recorded or synthesized voice message" [1]. Nora dials from a list and speaks a synthesized voice. Treat it as an ADAD.
- ADAD solicitation calls need prior express consent. You must be able to show the person authorized ADAD calls to that specific number [2].
- A pitch, a demo offer or "can I book 15 minutes" is solicitation.
- No consent is needed only for non-solicitation calls [2]. A sales call is not that.
- Status: CRTC consultation 2026-132 (opened June 11 2026) asks whether the ADAD definition should name AI voices, and about AI disclosure [3][4]. Comments closed Aug 11 2026. No decision found as of today. The direction is toward more coverage of AI, not less.
- Penalty exposure: up to $15,000 per violation for a corporation [5].

## 3. Telemarketing Rules: apply to any B2B call, human or AI

- Say who you are and who the call is for at the start.
- Hours: 9:00 to 21:30 weekdays, 10:00 to 18:00 weekends, the called person's local time.
- Show a callable originating number.
- Keep an internal do-not-call list. Act on a request during the call, add within 14 days, keep 3 years and 14 days.
- Give a contact number, name or address on request.

## 4. PIPEDA: probably fine

- Business contact information (name, title, work phone) used only to reach someone about their job is outside PIPEDA Part 1 (s.4.01) [6].
- A published number is still personal information in general. The business contact carve-out is the stronger route, so keep the use strictly work related [6][7].
- Other duties still apply: safeguards, honour opt-outs, no sale of the list.
- Quebec: Law 25 has its own regime. LAWYER before calling Quebec contacts.

## 5. CASL

- Does not cover voice calls. It does cover any follow-up email or SMS. Those need consent or the conspicuous publication exemption (published address, no "no CEMs" statement, message relevant to their role), plus ID and unsubscribe.

## What does work

| Option | AI voice? | Consent needed |
|---|---|---|
| A. Human cold call (Chris or Fred) | No | No. Telemarketing Rules only |
| B. Email or LinkedIn first, exec books a call or ticks "OK for an AI assistant to call me" | Yes, after opt-in | Yes, keep a record (who, number, date, wording) |
| C. Inbound: exec calls Nora's number or requests a demo by form with consent box | Yes | Inbound needs none. Outbound callback needs the box |
| D. Nora cold calls the exec | Yes | Not allowed without consent. Do not do |

Recommended: B. The consent line can be one checkbox: "Yes, Stewardli's AI assistant Nora may call me at this number about Stewardli."

If Nora calls under B or C:
- Open with: "Hi, this is Nora, an AI assistant calling for Stewardli. You asked us to call about ..."
- Honour "stop" in the call. Write to the internal DNC list the same day.
- Call recording notice if Retell records.
- Log consent proof in GHL (field: ai_call_consent, date, source).
