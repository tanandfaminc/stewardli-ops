## 5. Five-point checklist for Stewardli pilot clients
Plain English. PIPEDA-aware. Not legal advice (inferred: have counsel review).
1. Who owns the data. The charity owns its donor data. Stewardli only uses it to make their calls. Say it in writing.
2. Why we use it. Donor data is used only for thank-you and reminder calls for that charity. No other use. No selling or sharing.
3. No training on your donors. Donor data is not used to train shared AI models. Get this in writing from Retell, Twilio and any LLM vendor too (inferred: confirm each vendor's terms).
4. Donors can say no. The charity confirms donors agreed to be contacted by phone. Every call offers an easy opt-out. Opt-outs are honoured fast. Keep the Canadian Do Not Call and CRTC rules in mind (inferred: confirm applicability to charities).
5. Keep it small and delete it. Only import fields needed for the call. No call recordings kept longer than agreed. Delete data at pilot end or on request. Tell the client where data is stored, and that some vendors may process it in the US. Report any breach to the client right away.

Also: say plainly on the call that it is an AI voice (inferred best practice).
